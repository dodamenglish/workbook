20번 필자의 주장 · 습관과 편리성
People often ask me, "What surprises you most about habits?" One thing that continually astonishes me is the degree to which we're influenced by sheer convenience. The amount of effort, time, or decision making required by an action has a huge influence on habit formation. To a truly remarkable extent, we're more likely to do something if it's convenient, and less likely if it's not. For this reason, we should pay close attention to the convenience of any activity we want to make into a habit. Putting a wastebasket next to our front door made mail sorting slightly more convenient, and I stopped procrastinating with this chore. Many people report that they do a much better job of staying close to distant family members now that tools like group chats make it easy to stay in touch. 💬 핵심 요약: 우리는 편리한 것은 더 하고, 불편한 것은 덜 한다. 따라서 습관으로 만들고 싶은 행동의 편리성에 주의를 기울여야 한다.
1 내용 이해 — 빈칸 채우기
필자는 습관 형성에서 가장 놀라운 것이 의 영향력이라고 말한다. 우리는 어떤 행동이 하면 더 잘 하게 되고, 그렇지 않으면 덜 하게 된다. 따라서 필자는 습관으로 만들고 싶은 행동의 에 주의를 기울여야 한다고 주장한다. 예시로, 현관 옆에 을 두었더니 우편물 정리를 더 잘하게 되었다고 한다.

21번 밑줄 함축 의미 · 믿음과 행동의 관계 [3점]
It is common sense that people's inner beliefs may drive their external behavior. But beliefs and behaviors are also related in a more remarkable way. It turns out that the arrow is as likely to point in the reverse direction. As social psychologist David Myers observes, "If social psychology has taught us anything during the last 25 years, it is that we are likely not only to think ourselves into a way of acting but also to act ourselves into a way of thinking." 💬 핵심 요약: 일반적으로 믿음이 행동을 만든다고 생각하지만, 반대로 행동이 믿음을 만들 수도 있다. "화살표가 반대 방향을 가리킨다" = 행동 → 믿음 방향
1내용 이해 — 빈칸 채우기
상식적으로 사람의 내면의 이/가 외부 행동을 이끈다. 그런데 흥미롭게도, 도 반대로 을 만들 수 있다. 즉 우리는 '생각하며 행동하는 방식으로 들어갈' 뿐만 아니라, '하며 생각하는 방식으로도 들어갈' 수 있다.

22번 글의 요지 · 침묵과 경청
Imagine following the spirit of a silence vow into daily life. Challenge yourself to spend an entire day saying only what you absolutely must say. It's been widely observed that we too often tend to treat "conversation" as a game of waiting for our own turn to speak. We miss what's being said because we're mentally rehearsing our next utterance. What if you were limited to fifty spoken words tomorrow? I think you'd listen quite differently. You'd attend quite carefully to every word you heard. You might discover that the less you say, the more you hear. 💬 핵심 요약: 우리는 대화를 자기 차례를 기다리는 게임으로 여긴다. 말을 적게 할수록 상대방의 말을 더 잘 들을 수 있다.

23번 글의 주제 · 과학 지식과 행동
Science is concerned with accumulating and understanding observations of the physical world. That understanding alone solves no problems. Individual people have to act on that understanding for it to help solve problems. For instance, science has found that regular exercise can lower your risk of heart disease. Knowing this fact is interesting, but it will do nothing for your personal health unless you act on it and actually exercise. In this sense, science really solves no problems at all. Problems are only solved when people take the knowledge provided by science and use it. In fact, many of humanity's biggest problems are caused by lack of action, and not lack of knowledge. 💬 핵심 요약: 과학적 이해(지식)만으로는 문제가 해결되지 않는다. 실제로 행동해야 문제가 풀린다. 인류의 큰 문제는 지식 부족이 아니라 행동 부족에서 온다.
1내용 이해 — 빈칸 채우기
과학은 을 축적하고 이해하는 학문이다. 하지만 이해만으로는 문제가 되지 않는다. 예를 들어, 운동이 심장병 위험을 낮춘다는 사실을 알아도 실제로 하지 않으면 소용없다. 인류의 큰 문제는 지식의 부족이 아니라 의 부족에서 비롯된다.

24번 글의 제목 ⭐ · 현실 해석의 주관성
We think we're being logical, objective, and rational — and therefore accurate in our analysis. So we think that if other people are logical, they will agree with us and see what we see. But the opposite is the case. Every human brain is different. Everyone's life experience is different. You might think you're being realistic, but that's impossible. It's only your interpretation of reality, which will always be different from someone else's. When two nations play each other in the World Cup, the fans of each country criticize the referees for missing all the infractions that the other team commits. Without fail, each fan base believes that the referees are biased against their team. 💬 핵심 요약: 우리는 논리적이라고 생각하지만, 현실은 모두에게 다르게 해석된다. 같은 상황도 사람마다 다르게 본다 → '현실은 해석의 문제'

29번 어법 · 전문가의 지식 구조 [3점]
Studies of experts provide insight into what it means to have deep and flexible understanding. Experts in a particular domain are people who have deep, richly interconnected ideas about the world. They are not just good thinkers or people who are exceptionally smart. Rather, experts have knowledge in a specific domain and are not generalists. Having expertise in a topic means that knowledge is organized into coherent frameworks, and the expert understands the inter-relationship between facts. This kind of deep but organized understanding allows for greater flexibility in learning and facilitates application across multiple contexts. 💬 핵심 요약: 전문가는 단순히 똑똑한 것이 아니라, 특정 분야의 지식을 체계적인 틀로 조직하고 사실 간의 관계를 파악하는 사람이다.

30번 어휘 · 생태학적 오류 (이민자와 범죄율)
Sometimes reasoning is wrong because of misconceptions. One of these is the ecological fallacy, where an argument claims a causal relationship between two things merely because they co-occur. In the 1950s, crime rates were highest in neighborhoods where immigrants were most numerous. Some people argued that immigrants were a cause of crime. But analysis revealed that immigrants were forced to live in neighborhoods where crime rates were already high (not low); they could not afford safer housing. Immigrants themselves committed very few crimes. 💬 핵심 요약: 두 현상이 함께 일어났다고 해서 인과관계가 있는 건 아니다 (생태학적 오류). 이민자들은 원래 범죄율이 높은 동네에 살 수밖에 없었던 것.
1내용 이해 — 빈칸 채우기
생태학적 오류란, 두 현상이 한다는 이유만으로 둘 사이에 관계가 있다고 주장하는 오류이다. 1950년대 이민자와 범죄율 예시: 이민자들은 범죄율이 이미 동네에 살도록 되었던 것이며, 범죄의 원인이 아니었다.

31번 빈칸 추론 · 경험과 주의 집중 [3점]
In everyday life, we use previous experience to predict where we should pay attention. Different environments create different expectations. Jared Diamond wandered through the New Guinea jungle with native New Guineans. These natives can detect the most subtle changes in the jungle — they know which insects to leave alone, where food exists, and can build shelters with ease. Diamond, who had never spent time in such places, has no ability to pay attention to these things. 39번 문장 삽입 · 다이빙 반사 (Diving Reflex)
We have a 'diving reflex', like other marine mammals. Special nerve endings on our faces trigger this reflex only when the facial region goes under water. If our head is out in the air, there is no diving reflex. [삽입 위치: ③] But if we sink just our face in a bowl of water, while the whole of the rest of our body is in the dry air, the diving reflex is triggered. It automatically closes down the airway, reduces swallowing risk, and narrows the small air-passages in the lungs. At the same time, the heart rate slows to half speed and blood is shunted to the vital organs, protecting them from the brief stop in breathing. By contrast, if a chimpanzee found itself with its face below the surface, it would panic, its heart would race, and it would quickly drown. 💬 핵심 요약: 인간(및 해양 포유류)은 얼굴이 물에 닿으면 자동으로 다이빙 반사가 작동하여 기도를 닫고 심박수를 낮춰 생존을 돕는다. 침팬지는 이 반사가 없어 익사한다.
📌 정답: ③ — "얼굴이 공기 중이면 반사 없음" 뒤에, "그러나 얼굴만 물에 담그면 반사 작동"이 이어지는 자리

반사가 작동하면 가 닫히고 심박수가 으로 느려진다.
침팬지는 이 반사가 없어서 얼굴이 물에 잠기면 한다.

40번 요약문 빈칸 · 진실 편향 (Truth Bias)
There is a natural truth bias when humans communicate. Our automatic assumption is that the other person is telling the truth. Questioning the truth and then choosing not to believe it requires additional mental steps. For the most part, humans are "cognitive misers" — we typically don't expend more mental effort than seems necessary. When we see something online, even if it is fake, our default is to believe it, at least at first. 💬 핵심 요약: 인간은 자동으로 상대방이 진실을 말한다고 가정한다. 의심하려면 추가적인 정신적 노력이 필요한데, 인간은 '인지적 구두쇠'라서 그 노력을 아끼려 한다.

41~42번 장문 독해 · 신용카드와 소비 심리
Paying with plastic fundamentally changes the way we spend money. When you buy with cash, the purchase involves an actual loss — your wallet is literally lighter. Credit cards make the purchase abstract, so you don't really feel the downside of spending. Brain-imaging experiments suggest that paying with credit cards reduces activity in the insula, a brain region associated with negative feelings. "The nature of credit cards ensures that your brain is anesthetized against the pain of payment." Spending money doesn't feel bad, so you spend more.

In a sealed-bid auction for Celtics tickets, the average credit card bid was twice as high as the average cash bid. Credit card users were much more careless (not 'careful') — they no longer felt the need to limit their expenses. 💬 핵심 요약: 신용카드는 지출의 '고통'을 마비시켜 더 많이 쓰게 만든다. 경매 실험에서 신용카드 입찰가가 현금의 2배였음. (e) 'careful'이 아닌 'careless'가 맞음.
