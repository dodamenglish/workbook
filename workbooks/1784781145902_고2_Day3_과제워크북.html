지문 1 · 어법
One well-known shift took place when the accepted view — ①that the Earth was the center of the universe — changed to one where we understood that we are only inhabitants on one planet ②orbiting the Sun. With each person who grasped the solar system view, it became ③easier for the next person to do so. So it is with the notion that the world revolves around the human economy. This is slowly being replaced by the view that the economy is a part of the larger system of material flows that connect all living things. When this perspective shifts into place, it will be obvious that our economic well-being requires that we ④accounts for factors of ecological health. Unfortunately, we do not have a century or two ⑤to make the change.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?[d03h1a]
④ require는 요구동사이므로 that절에 (should) + 동사원형 → accounts → account. (주어 we와도 수 불일치.) ①the accepted view 뒤 동격 that + 완전한 문장(적절) ②planet을 능동으로 수식하는 현재분사(적절) ③became의 보어 비교급(적절) ⑤앞의 명사 a century or two를 수식하는 to부정사(적절).
이 글의 요지로 가장 적절한 것은?[d03h1b]
지동설로의 전환처럼, '세계가 인간 경제를 중심으로 돈다'는 생각도 '경제는 더 큰 물질 흐름 체계의 일부'라는 생태적 관점으로 바뀌고 있다 → ②. ①⑤는 지엽(좁)/무관(무), ③은 과장, ④는 반대(반).
지문 2 · 제목
Mental development consists of individuals increasingly mastering social codes and signals themselves, which they can master only in social situations with the support of more competent individuals, typically adults. In this sense, mental development consists of internalizing social patterns and gradually becoming a responsible actor. In Denmark, the age of criminal responsibility is 15, which means we can say that people have developed sufficient mental maturity to be accountable for their actions at this point. And at 18, people are given the right to vote. I do not know whether these age boundaries are optimal, but it is clear that mental development takes place at different rates for different individuals, and depends especially on the social and family environment they have been given. Therefore, having formal limits for responsibility from a specific age that apply to everyone is a somewhat questionable practice.
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d03h2a]
발달 속도가 사람마다 달라 일률적인 연령 기준은 의문스럽다는 글 → ⑤. ④는 본문과 반대(반), ②③은 덴마크 예시만 붙든 지엽(좁), ①은 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 optimal과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d03h2b]
optimal = 최적의, 가장 바람직한 → ideal. '이 연령 경계가 최적인지는 모르겠다'는 맥락. legal(합법적)은 글자만 스치는 함정.
지문 3 · 주제
People seem to recognize that the arts are cultural activities that draw on certain cultural traditions and values characteristic of the time and place in which the art is created. In the case of science, however, opinions differ. Some scientists, like the great biologist J. B. S. Haldane, see science in a similar light — as a historical activity that occurs in a particular time and place and needs to be understood within that context. Others, however, see science as a purely "objective" pursuit, uninfluenced by the cultural viewpoint and values of those who create it. In describing this view of science, philosopher Hugh Lacey speaks of the belief that there is an underlying order of the world which is simply there to be discovered — the world of pure "fact" stripped of any link with value.
이 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?[d03h3a]
예술이 문화적 활동이라는 데는 합의가 있으나, 과학이 문화적 맥락에서 자유로운지에 대해서는 견해가 갈린다(opinions differ) → ①. ④는 예시 인물만 붙든 지엽(좁), ②③⑤는 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 underlying과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d03h3b]
underlying order = 겉으로 드러나지 않은 근본적인 질서 → fundamental. '그저 발견되기를 기다리는' 질서라는 맥락이므로 visible(눈에 보이는)은 오히려 반대.
