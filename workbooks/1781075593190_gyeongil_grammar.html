Ch.01~02 | to부정사 / 동명사 — 명사적 용법 (주어·목적어)

핵심 개념
  • to부정사 주어To travel east is hard. → 단수 취급
  • 동명사 주어Traveling east shortens the cycle. → 단수 동사 주의!
  • 가주어 대체It is hard to travel east. → 실제로는 가주어 it 사용이 더 자연스러움
  • sense groupto부정사/동명사는 하나의 의미 덩어리 — 끊어 읽지 않음
⚠ 지문 연계
· Traveling west lengthens one's experience. (p.9 Jet Lag)
· Traveling east shortens one's experience. → 동명사 주어 = 단수 동사
· The act of driving is one of the best ways to prevent car sickness. (p.35)
✏️ 빈칸 채우기
1. west lengthens one's experience. (동명사 주어: 서쪽으로 여행하는 것)
2. It is our job as citizens sure that government does well. (to부정사)
3. The act of is one of the best ways to prevent car sickness. (동명사)
4. east shortens one's experience. (동명사 주어)
  1. Traveling
  2. to make
  3. driving
  4. Traveling

Ch.03 | 명사절 — that / whether / 의문사절

핵심 구분
  • that절(주어)It has been said that the biggest problem is ambiguity. → 가주어 it + 진주어 that절
  • that절(목적어)They claim that ambiguity helps us. → 동사 뒤 목적어
  • whether절(주어)Whether or not BPA should be banned is controversial.
  • 의문사절(목적어)They cannot determine exactly how the results were generated.
  • that절(동격)The concept suggests that every action has a consequence. (Karma)
⚠ 지문 연계
· It has been said that the biggest problem is ambiguity. (p.15 Ambiguity)
· concluded that the brain reacts similarly to both events. (p.29 TTS)
· Whether or not BPA should be banned is controversial. (p.29 BPA)
· suggests that every action has a consequence. (p.35 Karma)
✏️ 빈칸 채우기
1. It has been said the biggest problem in communication is ambiguity. (접속사)
2. The researcher concluded the brain reacts similarly to both events. (접속사)
3. or not BPA should be banned is controversial. (주어 명사절)
4. They cannot determine exactly the results were generated. (의문사)
5. They most likely thought were agreeing to let the Dutch use the land. (명사절 접속사)
  1. that
  2. that
  3. Whether
  4. how
  5. they (that they)

Ch.04 | 가주어 it — It ~ to-V / It ~ that절

핵심 구조
  • 기본It becomes easier to obtain the proper permissions. → it=가주어, to obtain...=진주어
  • 응용It would be more effective to have greater transparency. → AI Black Box 빈칸 정답 transparency
  • that절It has been said that the biggest problem is ambiguity.
  • 의미상주어It makes sense for speakers to use easy words. → for+목적격+to-V
⚠ 지문 연계 (빈칸 문제 핵심)
· it becomes easier to obtain permissions — accessible할 때 쉬워짐 (p.15 AI)
· it would be more effective to have greater [transparency] (p.15 AI 빈칸 정답)
· It makes sense for speakers to use "easy" words. (p.15 Ambiguity)
✏️ 빈칸 채우기
1. becomes easier to obtain the proper permissions. (가주어)
2. It would be more effective have greater transparency. (진주어 표지)
3. It makes sense for speakers use "easy" words. (to부정사)
4. It is our job as citizens make sure that government does what is expected. (진주어)
5. is surprising how many people experience car sickness. (가주어)
  1. It
  2. to
  3. to
  4. to
  5. It

핵심 형태 정리

was/were + p.p.
과거에 ~됐다
완료 수동
has/have been + p.p.
현재완료 수동
대과거 수동
had been + p.p.

수동+전치사

⚠ 지문 연계

4. People can be (expose) to BPA through their diet.
5. Petra (forget) for centuries when it was rediscovered.
6. The drama's scenes (film) in Seoul to attract foreign tourists. (현재 진행 수동)

Ch.07 | 조동사 + have + p.p. — 과거 사실에 대한 추측 / 후회

핵심 의미 정리
may have p.p.
~했을지도 모른다 (과거 추측)
must have p.p.
~했음에 틀림없다 (강한 추측)
cannot have p.p.
~했을 리 없다 (부정 추측)
should have p.p.
~했어야 했는데 (후회)
would have p.p.
~했을 것이다 (가정법 귀결)
could have p.p.
~할 수 있었을 텐데 (가정법)
⚠ 지문 연계 (Manhattan Bargain p.23)
· One of the most amazing bargains in history may have taken place in 1626. (과거 추측)
· If this legend is true, it would have likely caused a big misunderstanding. (가정법 귀결)
· The idea of "selling" their land would have been strange. (가정법 귀결)
✏️ 빈칸 채우기
1. One of the most amazing bargains in history (take place) in 1626. (과거 추측)
2. If this legend is true, it (cause) a big misunderstanding. (가정법 귀결)
3. The idea of selling their land (be) strange. (가정법 귀결)
4. You're late! You (get) up earlier. (후회: ~했어야 했다)
5. The thief (wear) gloves in order not to leave any fingerprints. (강한 추측 — 틀림없이)
6. I (complete) this project last Friday. (과거 능력 — 했을 수 있었을 텐데)
  1. may have taken place
  2. would have likely caused
  3. would have been
  4. should have gotten
  5. must have worn
  6. could have completed

Ch.08 | 동사와 함께 쓰이는 전치사

핵심 표현 (시험범위 지문 연계)
associate A with B
A를 B와 연관짓다
deprive A of B
A에게서 B를 빼앗다
discourage A from B
A가 B를 못하게 하다
credit A with B
A를 B의 공으로 돌리다
provide A with B
A에게 B를 제공하다
remind A of B
A에게 B를 상기시키다
distinguish A from B
A를 B와 구별하다
mistake A for B
A를 B로 오인하다
attribute A to B
A를 B의 결과/탓으로 돌리다
replace A with B
A를 B로 교체하다
⚠ 지문 연계
· Passengers associate acceleration with the sound of the engine. (p.35 Electric Car)
· Electric cars deprive us of this reference point. (p.35)
· They discourage passengers from looking outside. (p.35)
· Columbus is credited with discovering America. (p.13)
✏️ 전치사 빈칸 채우기
1. Passengers associate acceleration the sound of the engine. (with/of/from)
2. Electric cars deprive us this reference point. (of/from/with)
3. They discourage passengers looking outside. (from/of/with)
4. Columbus is credited discovering America. (with/of/for)
5. Doctors attribute the increase in skin conditions environmental factors. (to/from/with)
6. I sometimes mistake her her twin sister. (for/of/with)
7. Poetry provides us what is missing in our lives. (with/of/from)
  1. with
  2. of
  3. from
  4. with
  5. to
  6. for

Ch.09 | 관계사 — who / which / that / what / whose / 계속적 용법

핵심 구분
  • whoColumbus, who is credited with discovering America → 선행사: 사람
  • which(계속), which worsens the symptoms of car sickness → 앞 절 전체 받음. 앞에 콤마(,)
  • whatDutch settlers arrived in what is now the northeastern US → 선행사 포함 (=the thing that)
  • whoseshort, simple words whose meanings can be understood → 소유격 관계사
  • that 생략something (that) we learn from people around us → 목적격 관계사 생략
  • wherethe place where Petra is located → 장소 선행사
⚠ 지문 연계
· Dutch settlers arrived in what is now the northeastern US. (p.23 Manhattan)
· short, simple words whose meanings can be understood in context. (p.15 Ambiguity)
· Electric cars deprive us of this reference point, which worsens car sickness. (p.35 계속적)
· something (that) we learn from the people around us. (p.11 Legal, 목적격 생략)
✏️ 알맞은 관계사 쓰기
1. Dutch settlers arrived in is now the northeastern US. (선행사 포함 관계사)
2. short, simple words meanings can be understood in context (소유격 관계사)
3. Electric cars deprive us of this reference point, worsens car sickness. (계속적 관계사)
4. Ethics are simply something we learn from people around us. (목적격, 생략 가능)
5. This is a condition occurs when stress weakens the heart. (사물 선행사)
6. Columbus, is credited with discovering America, was an Italian explorer. (사람 선행사, 계속적)
  1. what
  2. whose
  3. which
  4. that (생략 가능)
  5. that/which
  6. who

Ch.10 | 간접의문문 — 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (평서문 어순)

직접 vs 간접 의문문 비교
  • 직접How well is the government doing? → 의문문 어순 (동사 앞)
  • 간접depends on how well the government is doing. → 평서문 어순 (주어+동사)
  • how+형용사how easy it is to make mistakes → "얼마나 쉬운지"
  • how+동사how the results were generated → "어떻게 생성됐는지"
  • what절what you paid them to do → "당신이 그들에게 하도록 돈을 낸 것"
⚠ 지문 연계
· depends on how well we think the government is doing. (p.29 Government — 주제문)
· cannot determine exactly how the results were generated. (p.15 AI Black Box)
· do what you paid them to do. (p.29 Government)
✏️ 어순 배열 → 올바른 간접의문문으로
1. depends on [well / the government / is / how / doing]
→ depends on
2. cannot determine [were / the results / generated / exactly / how]
→ cannot determine
3. Do you know [the unemployment rate / what / was / last year]?
→ Do you know ?
4. The manual explains [this device / how / works].
→ The manual explains .
  1. how well the government is doing
  2. exactly how the results were generated
  3. what the unemployment rate was last year
  4. how this device works

Ch.11 | 가목적어 it — S+V+it+형용사/명사+진목적어(to-V / that절)

핵심 구조
  • 기본Many people consider it impolite to point at others. → it=가목적어, to point=진목적어
  • 본문①This similarity makes it easy for vital functions to be affected. (BPA)
  • 본문②experts consider it unwise to use products that contain BPA. (BPA)
  • that절Customers find it convenient that the mall offers delivery service.
⚠ 지문 연계 (p.29 BPA)
· This similarity makes it easy for vital bodily functions to be negatively affected.
· many experts consider it unwise to use products that contain BPA.
✏️ 가목적어 it 빈칸 채우기
1. Many experts consider unwise to use products that contain BPA. (가목적어)
2. This similarity makes easy for vital bodily functions to be negatively affected. (가목적어)
3. I found difficult living in a foreign country. (가목적어)
4. Those who have a phobia of flying find impossible to relax on an airplane. (가목적어)
5. Customers find convenient that the online shopping mall offers a delivery service. (가목적어 + that절)

Ch.12 | 길이가 길거나 제 위치를 벗어난 목적어

핵심 패턴
  • A형(뒤로 이동)You can't imagine how much you are going to learn through this course. → 목적어(의문사절)가 긺
  • B형(앞으로 이동)What effect this trend will have on the future, scientists have yet to uncover. → 목적어가 문장 앞으로
  • C형(삽입)The writer ruined, [whether she intended to or not], the plot of the play. → 동사-목적어 사이 삽입절
  • 지문연계depends on how well we think the government is doing. → 전치사 뒤 의문사절
✏️ 다음 문장에서 목적어를 찾아 [ ]로 표시하고 해석하시오.
1. You must write in your cover letter what kind of career you're interested in.
→ 목적어:
2. Thomas Davenport will now describe for us how the information explosions have affected businesses.
→ 목적어:
3. The student president explained to her fellow students how she intended to improve the school.
→ 목적어:
4. How much effort we put into participating in government depends on how well we think the government is doing.
→ 주어: / 목적어(전치사 뒤):
  1. [what kind of career you're interested in]
  2. [how the information explosions have affected businesses]
  3. [how she intended to improve the school]
  4. 주어: [How much effort we put into participating in government] / 전치사 목적어: [how well we think the government is doing]
