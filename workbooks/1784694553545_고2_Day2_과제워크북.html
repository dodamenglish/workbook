지문 1 · 제목
Everything in the world exists on a continuum, whether in speed, size, or any other possible descriptor you could think of. Still, we create and mindlessly adopt sharp distinctions, and those distinctions change lives far more dramatically than marginal differences ever do. Indeed, all differences are arbitrary, but drawing hard lines between categories hides this and can be severely damaging. I call this resulting damage "the borderline effect." Someone's IQ is 69 and someone else's is 70 — but only the score of 70 is deemed to be within the range of normal. We don't have to be statisticians to know there is no meaningful difference between 69 and 70. Yet once the person with the lower score is labelled "cognitively impaired," his or her life will unfold quite differently.
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d02h1a]
연속선 위에 그은 자의적 경계선이 삶을 크게 바꾼다는 글 → ④. ①IQ 검사 폐지는 지엽(좁), ②·③은 언급 없음(무), ⑤는 '작은 차이가 경계선 때문에 큰 결과를 낳는다'는 본문과 반대(반).
밑줄 친 arbitrary와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d02h1b]
arbitrary = 이유가 아니라 임의로 정해진, 자의적인 → random. deliberate(의도적)·logical(논리적)·natural(자연스러운)은 오히려 반대쪽 뜻.
지문 2 · 제목
As you may already know, what and how you buy can be political. To whom do you wish to give your money, and which companies do you value and respect? Be mindful of every purchase by carefully researching the corporations that take our money, so as to decide whether they deserve our support. Do they have a record of polluting the environment, or do they practise fair trade? For instance, my family discovered a company that produces recycled, plastic-free toilet paper with a social conscience; it donates half its profits to building toilets around the world. Remember that the corporate world is built on consumers, so as a consumer you have the power to vote with your wallet and to encourage companies to embrace healthier, more sustainable practices.
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d02h2a]
'지갑으로 투표한다' = 기업을 조사해 의식적으로 구매하라 → ③. ①·⑤는 예시 하나만 붙든 지엽(좁), ②·④는 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 mindful과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d02h2b]
be mindful of ~ = ~에 주의를 기울이다 → attentive. 바로 뒤 'by carefully researching'이 단서. careless·forgetful은 반대.
지문 3 · 어휘
The "jolt" of caffeine does wear off. Caffeine is removed from your system by an enzyme within your liver, which gradually ①degrades it over time. Based in large part on genetics, some people have a more ②efficient version of the enzyme that degrades caffeine, allowing the liver to ③rapidly clear it from the bloodstream. These rare individuals can drink an espresso with dinner and fall fast asleep at midnight without a problem. Others, however, have a slower-acting version of the enzyme. It takes far longer for their system to eliminate the same amount of caffeine, so they are very ④insensitive to caffeine's effects. Aging also alters the speed of caffeine clearance: the older we are, the longer it takes our brain and body to remove caffeine, and thus the more ⑤sensitive we become in later life to caffeine's sleep-disrupting influence.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?[d02h3a]
④ 효소가 느려 카페인 제거에 오래 걸리면 카페인에 민감(sensitive)해진다. insensitive → sensitive. ①효소가 분해함 ②·③효율적 효소는 빠르게 제거 ⑤나이 들수록 제거가 느려져 더 민감 — 모두 적절.
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d02h3b]
카페인 제거 속도를 좌우하는 두 요인 = 유전과 노화 → ①. ④는 본문과 반대(반), ②·⑤는 언급 없음(무), ③은 효소 '생성'이라 지엽(좁).
