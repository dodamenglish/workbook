지문 1 · 어법
One well-known shift took place when the accepted view — ①that the Earth was the center of the universe — changed to one where we understood that we are only inhabitants on one planet orbiting the Sun. With each person ②whom grasped the solar system view, it became easier for the next person to do so. So it is with the notion that the world revolves around the human economy. This is slowly ③being replaced by the view that the economy is a part of the larger system of material flows. When this perspective shifts into place, it will be obvious that our economic well-being requires that we ④account for factors of ecological health. Unfortunately, we do not have a century or two ⑤to make the change.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?[d06h1a]
② 뒤에 동사 grasped가 바로 오므로 주격 관계대명사가 필요하다. whom → who. ①the accepted view 뒤 동격 that(적절) ③주어 This가 '대체되는' 대상이므로 수동 진행(적절) ④require + that절 → (should) 동사원형(적절) ⑤명사를 수식하는 to부정사(적절).
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d06h1b]
지동설로의 전환을 유비로 삼아, 경제 중심 세계관이 생태적 관점으로 바뀌고 있음을 말한다 → ⑤. ①④는 유비로 쓰인 예시만 붙든 지엽(좁), ③은 과장, ②는 반대(반).
지문 2 · 어법
All human cultures ①mark the passing of time by the differences they observe in the world around them. Our choice of which differences ②to mark ③depending firstly on what we can observe and secondly on what is important in our lives. In pre-modern agricultural Europe, ④where higher latitudes make the seasons easily observable, it was natural to monitor the solar cycle. Conversely, among the largely nomadic peoples of Arabia, for ⑤whom seasonal changes were less significant, the lunar calendar was a more sensible choice.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?[d06h2a]
③ 주어 Our choice에 대한 본동사가 필요한 자리인데 준동사(분사)가 왔다. depending → depends. ①복수주어 cultures→mark(적절) ②'which+명사+to부정사'(적절) ④장소 선행사 Europe + 완전한 절→where(적절) ⑤선행사가 사람(peoples)이므로 for whom(적절).
이 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?[d06h2b]
달력의 모양은 관찰 가능한 것과 삶에서 중요한 것 사이의 연결에서 나온다 → ①. ②는 한 요인만 떼어낸 지엽(좁), ③④는 언급 없음(무), ⑤는 우열을 가리지 않은 본문과 반대(반).
지문 3 · 주장
"Tactics" is a term borrowed from military usage: whereas strategies are plans of action that direct a force in attacking another, tactics are responses to conditions on the ground. In this vein, time is imposed on us — by our cultures, by technologies that have regimented it down to the nanosecond, and by its own finite nature. In response, we must develop tactics for dealing with time and waiting. These are not tactics for eliminating waiting from our lives altogether; rather, they are tactics for teaching us to learn from the seams. Such renewed perspectives transform waiting from a burden into a springboard toward creativity, social critique, and reflection.
이 글에서 필자가 주장하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?[d06h3a]
기다림을 없애는 전술이 아니라 그 틈에서 배우게 하는 전술 → 기다림을 도약대로 → ④. ①③은 본문과 반대(반), ②⑤는 어원 설명만 붙든 지엽(좁).
밑줄 친 springboard와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d06h3b]
springboard = 도약대, 발판 → launching point. 바로 앞의 'from a burden into a ~' 구조가 대조를 알려 주므로 obstacle·burden은 정반대.
