기초
수동태의 형태와 의미
주어 + be동사 + 과거분사(p.p.) + (by 행위자)
능동 Shakespeare wrote Hamlet.
수동 Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.
능동 They build the house.
수동 The house is built by them.
기초 — 수일치 & 시제
be동사 수일치 체크리스트
⚠️ 수동태로 바꿀 때 주어가 바뀐다!
능동태 목적어 → 수동태 주어 → 그 주어에 맞춰 be동사 결정
단수 + 현재 → is
The book is read…
복수 + 현재 → are
The books are read…
단수 + 과거 → was
The book was read…
복수 + 과거 → were
The books were read…
능동 They built these houses 100 years ago.
수동 These houses were built 100 years ago. ✓ 복수→were
중급 — by 생략
by + 행위자 생략 규칙
생략 ○
행위자가 일반인
people / someone / they
생략 ○
행위자 불분명/중요하지 않음
My bag was stolen.
생략 ✕
행위자가 특정인물
by Shakespeare, by King Sejong
생략 ✕
행위자 정보가 중요할 때
by a French family
수동 Korean is spoken in Korea. (by people 생략 ○)
수동 Hangeul was made by King Sejong. (생략 ✕ 특정인물)
심화
조동사 수동태 & 동사구 수동태
조동사 + be + p.p.
능동 He can repair the car.
수동 The car can be repaired by him.
능동 My sister looked after my cat.
수동 My cat was looked after by my sister.
4형식 수동태
능동 His mother made him a delicious cake.
수동① He was made a delicious cake by his mother.
수동② A delicious cake was made for him by his mother.
