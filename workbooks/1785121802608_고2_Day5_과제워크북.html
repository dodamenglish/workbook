지문 1 · 주장
Merely convincing your children that worry is senseless, and that they would be more content if they didn't worry, isn't going to stop them from worrying. For some reason, young people seem to believe that worry is a fact of life over which they have little or no control. Consequently, they don't even try to stop. Therefore, you need to convince them that worry, like guilt and fear, is nothing more than an emotion, and like all emotions, is subject to the power of the will. Tell them that they can eliminate worry from their lives by simply refusing to attend to it. Explain that if they refuse to act worried regardless of how they feel, they will eventually stop feeling worried and begin to experience the contentment of a worry-free life.
이 글에서 필자가 주장하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?[d05h1a]
걱정도 하나의 감정이며 의지의 힘에 종속된다(subject to the power of the will)고 설득하라 → ③. ②④는 본문과 반대(반), ①⑤는 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 eliminate와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d05h1b]
eliminate = 없애다, 제거하다 → remove. '걱정에 주의를 주지 않음으로써 삶에서 없앨 수 있다'는 맥락. hide(숨기다)는 없애는 것이 아니라 감추는 것이므로 함정.
지문 2 · 주장
"Tactics" is a term borrowed from military usage: whereas strategies are plans of action that direct a force in attacking another, tactics are responses to conditions on the ground. In this vein, time is imposed on us — by our cultures, by technologies that have regimented it down to the nanosecond, and by its own finite nature. In response, we must develop tactics for dealing with time and waiting. These are not tactics for eliminating waiting from our lives altogether; rather, they are tactics for teaching us to learn from the seams. Such renewed perspectives transform waiting from a burden into a springboard toward creativity, social critique, and reflection.
이 글에서 필자가 주장하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?[d05h2a]
전술은 기다림을 없애는 것이 아니라 그 틈(seams)에서 배우게 하며, 기다림을 부담에서 도약대로 바꾼다 → ④. ①③은 본문과 반대(반), ②는 어원 설명만 붙든 지엽(좁), ⑤는 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 imposed와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d05h2b]
be imposed on ~ = ~에게 강제로 부과되다 → forced upon. 문화·기술·시간의 유한성이 우리에게 시간을 강요한다는 맥락이므로 offered(제공)는 반대.
지문 3 · 어휘
The first human beings probably evolved in tropical regions where survival was possible without clothing. In time, they became capable of ①spreading out from Africa, eventually to most of the world. This was probably because their physical characteristics changed. For instance, early hominids probably did not walk upright, but when they developed that ability, they could travel more ②efficiently. More important, perhaps, was their development of tool making. With tools, they could hunt other animals, so they could consume more protein and fat than their low-energy vegetarian diet would have provided. Not only their bodies but also their brains would have been ③unchanged with more energy, since the brain needs lots of energy to grow. As their diet ④expanded, hominids could physically and intellectually ⑤expand their territory.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?[d05h3a]
③ 더 많은 에너지를 얻어 몸과 뇌가 바뀌었을 것이라는 흐름이고, 바로 뒤 'the brain needs lots of energy to grow'가 이를 뒷받침한다. unchanged → changed. ①아프리카에서 퍼져 나감 ②직립보행으로 더 효율적 이동 ④⑤식단 확대 → 영역 확장 — 모두 적절.
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d05h3b]
