지문 1 · 주제
If cooking is as central to human identity, biology, and culture as the biological anthropologist Richard Wrangham suggests, it stands to reason that the decline of cooking in our time would have serious consequences for modern life — and so it has. Are these consequences all bad? Not at all. The outsourcing of much of the work of cooking to corporations has relieved women of what has traditionally been their exclusive responsibility for feeding the family, making it easier for them to work outside the home and have careers. It has also relieved other pressures in the household and saved us time that we can now invest in other pursuits. It has even allowed us to diversify our diets substantially — all that's required is a microwave.
이 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?[d04h1a]
요리의 쇠퇴가 낳은 결과가 다 나쁘지는 않다(Not at all) — 여성의 부담 경감·시간 절약·식단 다양화 → ②. ①④는 언급 없음(무), ③⑤는 본문과 반대(반).
밑줄 친 substantially와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d04h1b]
substantially = 상당히, 크게 → considerably. '식단을 상당히 다양화했다'는 긍정적 결과의 나열 속에 있으므로 rarely·poorly 같은 부정어는 맥락과 어긋난다.
지문 2 · 어법
There is little doubt ①that we are driven by the sell-by date. Once an item is past that date it ②goes into the waste stream, further ③increasing its carbon footprint. But we all make our own judgement about sell-by dates; those brought up during the Second World War are often ④scornful of the terrible waste such caution encourages. The manufacturer of the food decides that a product can reasonably be consumed within, say, 90 days, and 90 days minus so many days for travelling gives the sell-by date. But whether it becomes toxic ⑤are something each individual can decide.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?[d04h2a]
⑤ 주어가 명사절 whether it becomes toxic(단수 취급)이므로 are → is. ①little doubt 뒤 동격 that+완전한 문장(적절) ②단수주어 it(an item)→goes(적절) ③분사구문 increasing(적절) ④be동사 보어 형용사 scornful(적절).
이 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d04h2b]
유통기한은 제조사의 계산일 뿐, 먹어도 되는지는 개인이 판단한다 → ③. ①은 반대(반), ②④는 곁가지 문장 하나만 붙든 지엽(좁), ⑤는 언급 없음(무).
지문 3 · 요지
The future of work depends on two forces: a harmful substituting force and a helpful complementing one. Many tales have a hero and a villain fighting each other for dominance, but in our story, technology plays both roles at once, displacing workers while simultaneously raising the demand for their efforts elsewhere in the economy. This interaction helps explain why past worries about automation were misplaced: our ancestors predicted the wrong winner, underestimating how powerful the complementing force would prove to be. It also helps explain why economists have traditionally been dismissive of technological unemployment: there appeared to be firm limits to the substituting force, leaving many tasks that machines could not perform and a growing demand for humans to do them instead.
이 글의 요지로 가장 적절한 것은?[d04h3a]
기술은 대체하는 힘과 보완하는 힘을 동시에 지닌다(plays both roles at once) → ④. ①은 반대·과장, ②⑤는 과장/반대, ③은 언급 없음(무).
밑줄 친 displacing과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d04h3b]
displace = (~의 자리를) 대신하다, 밀어내다 → replacing. 바로 앞의 'substituting force'가 결정적 단서. hiring·protecting은 정반대.
