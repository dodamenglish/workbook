지문 1 · 요지
Any new or threatening situation may require us to make decisions, and this requires information. So important is communication during a disaster that normal social barriers are often lowered. We will talk to strangers in a way we would never consider normally. Even relatively low-grade disruption of our life, such as a fire drill or a very late train, seems to give us permission to break normal etiquette and talk to strangers. The more important an event is to a particular public, the more detailed and urgent the requirement for news becomes. Without an authoritative source of facts, rumours often run riot.
이 글의 요지로 가장 적절한 것은?[d01h1a]
재난 시 정보 욕구로 사회적 장벽이 낮아지고(barriers lowered), 공신력 있는 출처가 없으면 소문이 퍼진다(rumours run riot) → ②. ①④는 본문과 반대, ③⑤는 언급 없음/지엽.
밑줄 친 authoritative와 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d01h1b]
authoritative = 권위 있는·믿을 만한 → reliable. 소문(rumours)과 대비되는 '공신력 있는' 출처를 뜻한다.
지문 2 · 어휘
The first human beings probably evolved in tropical regions where survival was possible without clothing. In time, they became capable of spreading out from Africa, eventually to most of the world. This was probably because their physical characteristics changed. For instance, early hominids probably did not walk upright, but when they developed that ability, they could travel more efficiently. More important, perhaps, was their development of tool making. With tools, they could hunt other animals, so they could consume more protein and fat than their low-energy vegetarian diet would have provided. Not only their bodies but also their brains would have been changed with more energy, since the brain needs lots of energy to grow.
밑줄 친 consume과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은?[d01h2a]
consume = (음식을) 섭취하다 → ingest. produce(생산)·waste(낭비)·avoid(피하다)는 반대/무관, digest(소화)는 섭취 이후의 다른 단계.
이 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?[d01h2b]
도구 → 고기 섭취 → 더 많은 에너지 → 신체·두뇌 변화 → 영역 확장. ⑤. ①②③은 본문과 반대, ④는 언급 없음.
지문 3 · 어법
All human cultures ①mark the passing of time by the differences they observe. Our choice of which differences to mark ②depends firstly on what we can observe. In pre-modern agricultural Europe, ③where higher latitudes make the seasons easily observable, it was natural to monitor the solar cycle. Conversely, among the largely nomadic peoples of Arabia, for ④which seasonal changes were less significant, the lunar calendar was a more sensible choice. That did not make it inevitable ⑤that Islam would use a lunar calendar.
밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?[d01h3a]
④ 선행사가 사람(the nomadic peoples)이고 뒤 절이 완전하므로 for which → for whom. ①복수주어 cultures→mark(적절) ②단수주어 choice→depends(적절) ③장소 선행사+완전한 절→where(적절) ⑤inevitable 뒤 진주어 명사절→that(적절).
이 글에서 달력의 형태를 결정하는 요인으로 가장 적절한 것은?[d01h3b]
관찰 가능한 차이 + 삶에서 중요한 것(생활방식)의 연결이 달력 형태를 결정한다. ①. 위도·종교·기술은 지엽적 요인일 뿐(본문: filtered through tradition).
